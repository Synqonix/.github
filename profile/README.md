## 🚀 About Synqonix

Synqonix is a unified productivity and collaboration platform designed to help teams manage work, organize projects, and streamline workflows.

Our mission is simple: reduce fragmentation and bring everything into sync.

## ✨ What We're Building

- **Project Management** — Organize projects, tasks, and priorities.
- **Team Collaboration** — Keep teams and their work connected.
- **Workflow Management** — Streamline day-to-day processes.
- **AI-Powered Productivity** — Help teams work more efficiently.
- **Self-Hosting** — Run Synqonix on your own infrastructure.
